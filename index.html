01 ABOUT

BUILT AROUND THE NEEDS OF HOUSING SOCIETIES

Professional support for communities that matter.

Samarth Circle works with cooperative housing societies and residential communities to bring structure, clarity and professional support to the responsibilities of community management.

Our work is centred on the practical realities faced by managing committees and residents — from statutory compliance and documentation to accounts, administration and governance.
